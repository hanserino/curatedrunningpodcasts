About this application

Best Running Podcasts is a free web application published by NEDA. This app helps runners discover hand-curated running podcasts, filter shows by topic and language, listen to recent episodes in the browser, star favorites for OPML export, and resume listening where they left off.

Application features: podcast directory, on-site episode player, favorites export, continue listening, and optional cross-device sync when signed in.

Why we request Google user data: You may optionally sign in with Google to sync listen progress, continue listening, and starred podcasts across browsers and devices. We use Google OAuth through Supabase to identify your account (typically your email address and basic profile information). We do not receive or store your Google password.

We only use Google user data to restore your listening library on other devices. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
