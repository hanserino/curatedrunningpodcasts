Runners' podcast picks

Elite runners and people in the sport share their five favorite podcasts — running-related or not — with notes on why each one is worth a listen.

These lists are guest-curated: each profile is someone we admire in the sport, sharing what they actually listen to — on easy days, travel days, and recovery days. Not every pick is about running, and that is the point.

CD

Courtney Dauwalter

Ultrarunner

American ultrarunner known for big-mile days and a calm, curious approach to suffering. Three-time winner of the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc and a fixture on the global ultra circuit.

@therealeagle (opens in a new tab)

Top 5 picks

  1. 1

    Talk Ultra

    Ian has been covering this world forever. When I want the pulse of trail and ultra without fluff, this is where I start.

  2. 2

    Science Vs

    Short, skeptical, and funny — perfect for a cooldown jog when my brain still wants something to chew on.

  3. 3

    How I Built This

    Stories about people who stuck with an idea. That resonates on long training blocks when progress feels invisible.

  4. 4

    KoopCast

    Jason breaks down training and fueling in a way that respects how hard 100-mile weeks actually are.

  5. 5

    The Daily

    Twenty minutes of context before the day starts. I listen more than I admit on rest days.

KJ

Kilian Jornet

Mountain athlete

Ski mountaineer, sky runner, and record setter across the Alps and Himalaya. Trains and races at altitude more than most people commute.

@kilianj (opens in a new tab)

Top 6 picks

  1. 1

    Science of Ultra

    When I want physiology without hype — how the body adapts to volume and altitude — this is the show I trust.

  2. 2

    The Real Science of Sport

    Ross and Mike question popular training ideas. I like that they leave room for nuance.

  3. 3

    Stuff You Should Know

    Random topics, easy pace. Good company on easy recovery runs when the legs are tired but the mind is awake.

  4. 4

    Ten Junk Miles

    Honest conversations with people who live in the sport — not just the headlines.

  5. 5

    Between Two Pines

    Trail stories with heart — one of the shows I come back to when I want something grounded and human.

  6. 6

    Radiolab

    Sound design and storytelling that makes a long run feel shorter. One of the first podcasts I ever saved.

DL

Des Linden

Marathoner

Two-time Olympian and 2018 Boston Marathon champion. Known for straightforward talk about training, consistency, and showing up on hard days.

@des_linden (opens in a new tab)

Top 5 picks

  1. 1

    Strength Running Podcast

    Jason keeps it practical — strength, mileage, and the boring stuff that actually works for road runners.

  2. 2

    The Extramilest Show

    Long-form chats with people who have been in the sport a long time. I pick these up on treadmill days.

  3. 3

    Serial

    Old episodes still hold up. A good story makes an hour on the treadmill disappear.

  4. 4

    Breaking Marathon Limits

    Marathon-specific without pretending every week is a peak week. That honesty matters.

  5. 5

    SmartLess

    Three friends who clearly enjoy each other. Light and funny when I need a break from thinking about splits.
