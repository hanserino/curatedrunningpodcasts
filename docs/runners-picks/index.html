Andy Jones-Wilkins

Ultrarunner and podcaster

10 Time Western States 100 finisher, iRunFar columnist and host of Crack A Brew With AJW podcast

@ajoneswilkins (opens in a new tab)

Top 5 picks

  1. 1

    Trail Runner Nation

    Great hosts who don’t take themselves or running too seriously

  2. 2

    Conversational Pace

    Really good, in depth shoe reviews

  3. 3

    Distance to Empty

    Fantastic focus on 200 mile plus distances

  4. 4

    KoopCast

    Really good training advice based on science

  5. 5

    Singletrack

    All the news on the elite side of the sport

Kilian Jornet

GOAT trail runner and mountain athlete

Catalan mountain athlete living in Norway, widely regarded as one of the greatest trail and ultrarunners of all time, known for winning major races and setting records in extreme mountain environments. He has also achieved remarkable feats in ski mountaineering and high-altitude mountaineering, including record-setting ascents of peaks such as Mount Everest.

Aaron Lutze

Mountain biker, podcast host and YouTuber

Mountain biker, content creator, and former professional rider best known for his “Super Rider” media platform. He is also the co-founder and co-host of Second Nature, a podcast and newsletter focused on the business of the outdoor industry together with his co-host Dylan Bowman, who you may know from the Freetrail podcast.

@aaronlutze (opens in a new tab)

Top 5 picks

  1. 1

    Acquired

    This is like an audiobook and podcast rolled into one - deep dives, but well worth your time

  2. 2

    The Colin and Samir Show

    Interviews and breakdowns of the Creator economy, this is inside baseball for all content nerds like me

  3. 3

    My First Million

    An entire business podcast built around four words “have you seen this?”

  4. 4

    The Freetrail Podcast

    Proud to see what Dylan has built - and this podcast’s evolution (and contribution) in the space

  5. 5

    How I Built This

    Inspiring stories from successful founders, this is the OG of business podcasts if you ask me

Jeff Pelletier

Canadian trailrunner, filmmaker and podcast host

A prominent Vancouver-based ultra trail runner and documentary filmmaker. Known for tackling grueling multi-day and point-to-point endurance events worldwide, he documents his journeys in cinematic, first-person style films on his Jeff Pelletier YouTube Channel. He’s also the host of the podcast Creators Cooldown where he speaks to some of the top creators of running related content, from filmmakers and photographers, to writers and podcast hosts.

@jpelletier (opens in a new tab)

Top 5 picks

  1. 1

    Singletrack

    The best source for news on the professional side of the sport, along with really insightful interviews

  2. 2

    The Freetrail Podcast

    Great race previews and other stories from the sport

  3. 3

    Second Nature

    The business side of running and outdoor

  4. 4

    KoopCast

    The science of training and racing

  5. 5

    Some Work, All Play

    A lighter approach, with some great insights into training and physiology
