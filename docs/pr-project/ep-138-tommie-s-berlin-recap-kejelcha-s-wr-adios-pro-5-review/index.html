About this episode

How did Tommie fare on the legendary streets of Berlin?

In this episode, we get a full Berlin Marathon recap, break down Yomif Kejelcha’s new Half Marathon World Record, and review the highly-anticipated adidas adizero Adios Pro 5!

The PR Project crew is back, and we are kicking things off with a massive shoutout to our new sponsor, Precision Fuel and Hydration! After some banter about how proud we actually are of our diets and a brief history of MSG, we dive straight into Tommie’s official Berlin Marathon recap. Tommie breaks down his battle with returning cramps and explains how his ultra-running background ultimately helped him push through 26.2 miles.

On the pro side, we discuss Vincent Mauri’s crazy Chicago Marathon training block and how he stacks up against Clayton Young. We also react to Yomif Kejelcha’s blazing Half Marathon World Record and his outlook for Valencia, plus the latest announcements around Nike Apex and SwooshFly. In Run Culture Court, we debate the ethics of running through a cemetery and call out runners who claim they "can't run slow paces." Finally, we wrap up with a full shoe review of the adidas adizero Adios Pro 5!
