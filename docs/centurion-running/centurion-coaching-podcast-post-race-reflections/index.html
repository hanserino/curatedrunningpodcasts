About this episode

Centurion coaches Robbie Britton and Sarah Cameron talk through the role of post-race reflection, why and when it’s best done and give tips on how to learn the most from your recent races.

To finish they give a practical example of a coach and athlete reflecting together on Robbie’s recent race at the 135km Quebec Mega Trail as part of the World Trail Majors.
