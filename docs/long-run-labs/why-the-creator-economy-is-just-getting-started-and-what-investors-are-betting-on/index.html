Why the Creator Economy Is Just Getting Started and What Investors Are Betting On

Long Run Labs · June 23, 2026

About this episode

Megan Lightcap runs the creator fund at Slow Ventures, one of the first venture funds built specifically around creator entrepreneurs. Her thesis is simple: creators are just a new type of founder. And the ones who own a niche, build a world around themselves, and treat their audience like a community rather than an audience are the ones who will build empires.

Megan joins Long Run Labs to break down the creator landscape in 2026, why individuals are beating brands, what AI is actually doing for creators right now, and where she sees the biggest opportunities being overlooked.

Jon and Megan chat about:

  • why people matter and brands don't in 2026

  • own the niche not the category and what that looks like in practice

  • the difference between a creator and a creator founder

  • why haters are actually a good signal when evaluating creators to back

  • how AI is washing away undifferentiated creators and accelerating the rest

  • the AI boot camp Slow Ventures ran for 30 creators in San Francisco

  • building in public and why staying top of mind matters more than going viral

  • why eyeballs and follower counts are the lowest signal metrics in 2026

  • where venture capital is looking in the outdoor and trail running space

  • the fringe to mainstream pipeline and what categories are next

  • the wide open lane Megan is looking to fill around aging parents and end of life decisions
