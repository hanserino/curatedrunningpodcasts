Territorio Trail Podcast

Spanish-language trail running and mountain sports podcast from Aragón Radio, hosted by Alfonso García and Raúl Leorza. It combines in-depth interviews, race coverage, athlete stories, training insights, and news from the global trail-running scene, with a particular focus on major races such as Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc, Zegama-Aizkorri, and Marathon des Sables.
