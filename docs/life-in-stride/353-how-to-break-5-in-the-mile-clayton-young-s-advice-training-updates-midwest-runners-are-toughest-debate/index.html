On this episode, the boys had a surprise banter episode due to a time confusion with it guest and it turned out to be a pretty good episode! We gave training updates and Chris also shared the achilles advice he indirectly received from Clayton Young.

We did a bit of Midwest states trivia and debated more if Midwest runners are the toughest runners. Danny brought a case study to the podcast - he has a friend trying to break 5 in the mile but can only run 20 miles a week. Much more fun topics - enjoy!
