Trail Tales: The Short Trail Races We’re Most Excited for in 2026 + Comeback Updates & TERREX Camp

The Sub Hub · March 20, 2026

About this episode

In this week’s episode, Dani and EmKay share their top picks for short trail races around the world that they’re most excited about in 2026. They also dive into their personal progress through injury and pregnancy comebacks, and Dani reflects on her experience at the Adidas TERREX team camp hosted at Adidas Headquarters.
