Petter Engdahl on Versatility in Trail Running

The Sub Hub · May 29, 2026

About this episode

Dani and EmKay sit down with Petter Engdahl to chat about his recent switch from a UTMB focus to OCC. Petter has had success at both short and long trail distances, showing his incredible versatility in a sport with so many different paths. We hope you enjoy his perspective as much as we did.
