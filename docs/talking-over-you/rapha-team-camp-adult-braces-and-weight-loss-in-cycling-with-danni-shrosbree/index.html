Rapha team camp, adult braces, and weight loss in cycling (with Danni Shrosbree)

Talking Over You · April 29, 2026

About this episode

Sarah is in Spain at the Rapha training camp this week, so she sat down with Danni Shrosbree to talk about peeing (obviously), shoes, and adult braces. They also discuss the ongoing attention surrounding weight management in cycling, especially in the women’s peloton, and how it’s affected their own lives. Plus, since Hannah is off this week, Sarah takes the opportunity to roast her about her first Hotmail account.

Content warning: This episode contains discussion of weight loss and eating disorders.
