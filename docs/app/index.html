Best Running Podcasts

What Best Running Podcasts does, its features, and why optional Google sign-in is offered.

Best Running Podcasts is a free web application published by NEDA. Visit the podcast directory to browse shows.

What this application does

This app helps runners discover hand-curated running podcasts. You can:

  • browse and filter podcasts by topic and language
  • listen to recent episodes in the browser
  • star favorites and export them as OPML
  • resume listening where you left off

You can use all of this without an account. Data stays in your browser unless you choose to sign in.

Optional Google sign-in

You may optionally sign in with Google to sync listen progress, continue listening, and starred podcasts across browsers and devices.

Sign-in is handled by Supabase using Google OAuth. We use your Google account only to identify your library — typically your email address and basic profile information. We do not receive or store your Google password.

When signed in, we store:

  • listen progress for episodes you play
  • your continue-listening episode
  • podcast URLs you have starred

We use this data only to restore your experience on other devices. We do not sell it or use it for advertising profiles.
