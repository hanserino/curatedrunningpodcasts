Training the Gut for Ultramarathons with Isabel Martinez #241

KoopCast · April 24, 2025

About this episode

Isabel Martinez is a Research Sports Dietitian In the Department of Nutrition, Dietetics and Food at Monash University. She is the author of several studies on gut training as well as consults with athletes in this area.
