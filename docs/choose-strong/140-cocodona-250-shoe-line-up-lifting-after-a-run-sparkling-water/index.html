About this episode

In this episode we chat shoe rotation for the upcoming 250 mile race in AZ, some Sally Says Q & A and we touch on the taper that has begun in Flagstaff. And, as always, we touch on so many other things that you will for sure be entertained by and encouraged by. Thanks for listening!

Other episode highlights:

    • Mount to Coast Double Boston
    • Humanoid robot half-marathon world record

    • Navigating friendship change
