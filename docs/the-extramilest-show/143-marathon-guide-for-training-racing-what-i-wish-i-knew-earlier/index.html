About this episode

This episode is the advice I'd give a friend running their first marathon, or a friend who wants to run a faster one. I'm Floris Gierman, and I've run more than 50 marathons and ultras. I wish I'd known all of this all earlier in my running journey, because it would have saved me a lot of sore muscles, injuries, bonking and cramping.

In this video we cover long runs that prepare you for race day, marathon nutrition and electrolytes, how to slow down your easy runs, sleep and recovery, strength training for runners, how to pace a marathon, and the 10 most common marathon mistakes.
