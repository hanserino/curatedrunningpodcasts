#113: How to Finish Every Race Strong | Marathon Tips from My Sister's First Marathon

The Extramilest Show · November 20, 2025

About this episode

My new book Running Breakthroughs just launched and is available as audiobook, hardcover, paperback and ebook. Get incredible Book Launch Bonuses when you order now at https://florisgierman.com

I sat down with my sister Janneke to reflect on her journey from being injury-prone and inconsistent to running her first marathon with a 5 minute negative split in 3:53 (1:59 first half, 1:54 second half). We discuss how to run a negative split, finish a race strong every time, low HR training, self belief and more.

Watch this full video on YouTube: https://youtu.be/-0S3QjiqZi8
