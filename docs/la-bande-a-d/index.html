La Bande à D+ Podcast

A French-language talk-show podcast about trail running and ultra-running, produced by the media outlet Distances+ and hosted by Nicolas Fréret. Each episode brings together elite athletes, coaches, scientists, and trail-running personalities to debate current topics, race results, training, performance, and trends in the trail and outdoor sports world.
