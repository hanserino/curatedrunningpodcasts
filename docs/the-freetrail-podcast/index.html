The Freetrail Podcast

American trail and ultrarunning podcast that features interviews with elite athletes, coaches, race directors, and other influential figures in the sport, alongside discussions of major races, training, and industry trends. Hosted by Dylan Bowman, aka "the voice of the sport", it focuses on the people, stories, and ideas shaping the global trail running community.
