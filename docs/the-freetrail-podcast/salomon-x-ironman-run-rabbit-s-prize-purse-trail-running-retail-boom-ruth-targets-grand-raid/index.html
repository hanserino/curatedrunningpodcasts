About this episode

Rest Day Live is back tonight with Corrine Malcolm in the co-host chair. Together we'll catch up on all the biggest news in trail running over the past few weeks.

The docket:

• Salomon x IRONMAN: Salomon's biggest move beyond trail

• Run Rabbit Results & Prize Money News: Hannah Allgood & Ryan Sullivan earn victories

• Trail Running Retail Boom: REI & Marathon Sports see growth in trail market

• Ruth Croft Targets Diagonale des Fous: The Kiwi legend pivots after late scratch at UTMB

• Mammoth Trailfest Preview

• Strava File of the Week

We'll be LIVE on YouTube starting at 5pm PT tonight.
