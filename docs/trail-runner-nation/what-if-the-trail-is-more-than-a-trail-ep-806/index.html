About this episode

Accomplished ultrarunners Bob Crowley and Tim Twietmeyer return to Trail Runner Nation to explore an intriguing evolution of trail running: using our fitness not just to race, but to experience history on foot. What began as an attempt to retrace the Donner Party’s Forlorn Hope escape route grew into History Expeditions, combining meticulous historical research with trail runners' ability to reach remote places and test theories on the ground. Bob and Tim explain how standing on the terrain can reveal what books and maps miss, sometimes even challenging long-held assumptions about historical routes. The conversation also explores the remarkable endurance of people like the members of the Forlorn Hope and Snowshoe Thompson, whose feats make some modern endurance accomplishments look surprisingly comfortable. Most importantly, Bob and Tim argue that runners don't need to organize a massive expedition to try this themselves. A little curiosity, some local history, and a pair of running shoes can turn the trail you've run a hundred times into an entirely new adventure.
