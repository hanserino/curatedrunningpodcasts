Xampany o Suero

A Catalan-language mountain sports podcast focusing on trail running, ultrarunning, mountain culture, and endurance sports, with an emphasis on critical thinking, rigor, humor, and perspective. "Xampany" = champagne, "suero" = medical saline/IV fluid), a nod to the highs and lows of endurance sports—success and celebration versus suffering and recovery.
