## About this application

**Best Running Podcasts** is a free web application published by **[NEDA](https://www.naerdetalvor.no/)**. It helps runners discover hand-curated running podcasts, filter shows by topic and language, listen to recent episodes in the browser, star favorites for OPML export, and resume listening where they left off.

You can use the directory, player, and favorites **without creating an account**. Data stays in your browser unless you choose to sign in.

## Optional Google sign-in

You may optionally **sign in with Google** to sync listen progress, continue listening, and starred podcasts across browsers and devices. Sign-in is handled by **Supabase** using Google OAuth. We use your Google account only to identify your library — we do not receive or store your Google password.

When signed in, we store your listening progress, continue-listening episode, and favorite podcast URLs so you can restore them on other devices. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising profiles.

Read our **[Privacy Policy](/privacy/)** for how we access, use, store, and delete Google user data. See our **[Terms of Service](/terms/)** for rules on using the site.
